Openfolio Cloud

Privacy notice

Beta. Openfolio Cloud is an invite-only beta run by one person. This page says plainly what happens to your data; it will change as the service grows, and the date below will change with it.

Who runs this

Openfolio Cloud is run by Faisal Rehman.{% if contact_email %} Contact: {{ contact_email }}.{% endif %}

When you request access

We store what you type into the form: your name, email, portfolio address, what you do and, if you add it, why you want access. We also store the time and a hash of your IP address, salted with a server secret. The hash lets us limit spam (three requests per network per hour) without keeping the address itself.

We use these details only to review your request and email you about it. We don't sell them, share them for marketing or add you to a newsletter. The owner may get an email notification with your request, sent through our mail provider.

When you talk to an Openfolio AI

To answer, your voice or typed messages are sent to the AI providers that run the service: speech-to-text, a language model and a voice, as listed in the README. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing. Audio may be relayed through Cloudflare's TURN service, which carries the encrypted stream without being able to listen to it.

We don't save recordings or transcripts. Server logs, which our host keeps for a limited time, can contain short parts of a conversation, for example a message flagged as an attempt to misuse the AI. If you leave a message for someone through their AI, it's stored so they can read it.

Cookies and storage

Visitors get no cookies and no trackers. The only cookie is the owner's sign-in for the admin page. The demo widget remembers in your browser's session storage that you closed its greeting.

How long we keep it

  • Rejected requests are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Invite links that were never used are deleted 30 days after they expire. Links are stored only as a hash.
  • Approved requests are kept while you take part in the beta.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your details, a correction or deletion at any time{% if contact_email %} by emailing {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}. Deleting a request also deletes its invite links.
