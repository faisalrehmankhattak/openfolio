Invites

Invite links are personal. Each works once and expires after 7 days. Please don't share yours; ask us for another if it expires.

Using it fairly

  • Create an AI version of yourself only, from information that is true.
  • Don't use it to impersonate anyone, mislead visitors into thinking they're talking to a person, or break the law.

We may pause or remove access that breaks these rules.

Your content

What you write stays yours. You let us store and process it only to run the service for you, and you can delete it at any time.

AI can be wrong
