Safety

Four promises we build around

An AI that speaks for you has to be honest about what it is and careful with what it says.

  • {{ icon("tag", 22) }}

    Always labelled AI

    The button, the panel and the first answer all say it's an AI version of you. It never claims to be human.

  • {{ icon("book", 22) }}

    Answers only from what you wrote

    Facts come from your story alone. When something isn't covered, it says it doesn't know and points to the real you.

  • {{ icon("globe", 22) }}

    Only on your own site

    It runs on the websites you list. Other sites can't put your AI on their pages.

  • {{ icon("pause", 22) }}

    You can pause any time

    Switch it off and it stops answering. Your story stays yours, and you can delete it.
