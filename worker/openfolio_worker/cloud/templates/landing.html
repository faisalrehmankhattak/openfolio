How it works

From résumé to a portfolio that talks back

Start from what you already have. You stay in control of every word.

  1. {{ icon("upload", 22) }}

    Step 1

    Upload your résumé and photo

    A PDF résumé and one good photo are enough to begin.

  2. {{ icon("sparkle", 22) }}

    Step 2

    Your AI drafts itself

    Openfolio turns your résumé into a first draft: projects, experience, skills and suggested questions.

  3. {{ icon("eye", 22) }}

    Step 3

    Review every word

    Edit the facts, add your own rules such as "never discuss salary" and choose your 3D avatar. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

  4. {{ icon("share", 22) }}

    Step 4

    Share a link or paste one line

    Send your link to recruiters, or add one script tag to your own site.

    <script src="…/embed.js" data-persona="you" async></script>

Safety

Four promises we build around

An AI that speaks for you has to be honest about what it is and careful with what it says.

  • {{ icon("tag", 22) }}

    Always labelled AI

    The button, the panel and the first answer all say it's an AI version of you. It never claims to be human.

  • {{ icon("book", 22) }}

    Answers only from what you wrote

    Facts come from your story alone. When something isn't covered, it says it doesn't know and points to the real you.

  • {{ icon("globe", 22) }}

    Only on your own site

    It runs on the websites you list. Other sites can't put your AI on their pages.

  • {{ icon("pause", 22) }}

    You can pause any time

    Switch it off and it stops answering. Your story stays yours, and you can delete it.

Open source

Built in the open

Openfolio is open source under Apache-2.0. Read every line, run it on your own server, or let us host it for you.

  • {{ icon("server", 20) }}No GPU needed. The 3D face renders in the visitor's browser.

  • {{ icon("lock", 20) }}Nothing from other origins. The widget talks only to your server.
