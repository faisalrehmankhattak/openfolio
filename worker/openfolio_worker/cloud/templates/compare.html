When a static portfolio site is enough

  • Your work explains itself visually, and visitors rarely have follow-up questions.
  • You want full control of every pixel and no AI at all.
  • You are early in your career and still building material. Start here either way: an AI portfolio needs good material to draw on.

When a generic chatbot widget fits

  • You run a business site and need answers about products, orders or support.
  • You have a team to configure and monitor it.

When an AI portfolio is the better choice

  • Recruiters or clients visit your site to decide whether to talk to you.
  • Your work has context worth explaining: decisions, trade-offs and results.
  • You want to be reachable across time zones without being always on.
  • You want an AI that speaks for you only from your own words, and always says it's an AI.

Use them together

An AI portfolio doesn't replace your site. Openfolio adds a 3D AI version of you on top of the portfolio you already have, with one script tag, or gives you a shareable link if you don't have a site yet.
